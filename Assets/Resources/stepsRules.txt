<size=150%><b>Steps: Game Rules</b></size>

<size=120%><b>🎯 Objective</b></size>
The primary goal is to be the first player to capture <b>12 of your opponent's tiles</b>. 

<size=120%><b>🎲 Setup</b></size>
1. <b>Player 1</b> places their pawn on an empty space on the board.
2. <b>Player 2</b> then places their pawn on an empty space on the board.

<size=120%><b>🚶 Gameplay & Movement</b></size>
On your turn, you may move your pawn across the board. 
• <b>Valid Direction:</b> You can move to any of the 8 adjacent spaces.
• <b>Elevation Limits:</b> The space you move to must be exactly <b>one level higher</b> or <b>one level lower</b> than your current space.
• <b>Obstacles:</b> You cannot move onto a space occupied by the opponent's pawn or the opponent's tiles (unless you are making a capture).
• <b>Multi-Step Moves:</b> You may move multiple spaces in a single turn. 
• <b>Momentum Rule:</b> You cannot change vertical direction during a normal movement phase. If you start your turn by moving UP to a higher level, all subsequent moves that turn must also be UP. If you start moving DOWN, all moves must be DOWN. <i>(Exception: See Capturing)</i>.

<i>On your turn, you must either Capture or Build.</i>

<size=120%><b>⚔️ Action 1: Capturing Tiles</b></size>
• <b>How to Capture:</b> You capture your opponent's tiles by moving your pawn from an adjacent space that is at least one level higher <b>down</b> onto their tiles.
• <b>Collecting:</b> Take the captured tiles off the board and place them in front of you to keep score.
• <b>Turn Ends:</b> Your turn ends immediately after completing a capture.

<size=120%><b>🧱 Action 2: Building (If You Don't Capture)</b></size>
If you do not capture any of your opponent's tiles during your turn, you must build:
• <b>How Many to Build:</b> The number of tiles you place is equal to the number of spaces you moved during that turn.
• <b>Minimum Rule:</b> You must always place <b>at least 1 tile</b> at the end of your turn, even if you moved zero spaces or were unable to move.
• <b>Where to Build:</b> 
  • You can place tiles on empty squares on the board.
  • You can stack tiles on squares where you have previously placed your own tiles.
  • You <b>may</b> build directly under your own pawn.
  • You <b>may not</b> build under your opponent's pawn.

<size=120%><b>🏆 Winning the Game</b></size>
• <b>Standard Win:</b> The first player to successfully capture <b>12</b> of the opponent's tiles wins immediately.
• <b>Supply Exhaustion Win:</b> If the central supply of tiles runs out, the game ends. The player who has captured the most opponent tiles wins.
