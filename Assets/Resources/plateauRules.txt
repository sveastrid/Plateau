<size=150%><b>Plateau Rules</b></size>

A VR board game of plateaus, bridges, and gemhearts.

<color=#ffffff55>────────────────────────────────────────</color>

<size=120%><b>Board Layout</b></size>

• The board has <b>40 plateaus</b>, including one larger <b>central plateau</b>.
• Plateaus vary in color. The colors represent the percent chance of that color being chosen.
• A faint line between two plateaus means they can be <b>crossed by a bridge</b> or <b>jumped by a parshendi</b>.
• All players begin on the central plateau.

<color=#ffffff55>────────────────────────────────────────</color>

<size=120%><b>VR Interaction</b></size>

• Each player can see how many gemhearts they currently hold.
• Every piece sits on a colored square indicating its owner.
• Each piece displays a <b>number</b> above the piece showing how many of that piece type occupy that plateau.
• Players use a <b>pointer</b> to select pieces; the piece highlights when targeted.
• After selecting a piece stack on a plateau, the player uses the <b>joystick</b> to choose how many of those pieces to move.
• Plateaus that the selected piece type can legally move to are <b>highlighted</b>.

<color=#ffffff55>────────────────────────────────────────</color>

<size=120%><b>Starting Forces</b></size>

Each player begins with:

• <b>Bridges:</b> 2
• <b>Troops:</b> 6
• <b>Shardbearer:</b> 1
• <b>Parshendi:</b> 2

All starting pieces are placed on the central plateau. Before the game starts, players place their first bridge one at a time, then place the second in reverse order (so that the person who placed the first bridge first will place their second bridge last).

<color=#ffffff55>────────────────────────────────────────</color>

<size=120%><b>Buying Pieces</b></size>

• 1 gemheart = 2 bridges
• 1 gemheart = 6 troops
• 1 gemheart = 2 parshendi
• 2 gemhearts = 1 shardbearer

New pieces spawn on the <b>central plateau</b>.

<color=#ffffff55>────────────────────────────────────────</color>

<size=120%><b>Movement</b></size>

Each piece may move <b>once per turn</b>.

• <b>Bridge:</b> One bridge may be moved per turn. A bridge already on the board may be re-laid across any empty gap with one end in the group of plateaus it currently connects — that is, the plateaus reachable from either of its own ends over that player's bridges. A bridge placed from reserve is laid from the group connected to the central plateau.
• <b>Troop:</b> Crosses up to two bridges owned by that player per turn.
• <b>Parshendi:</b> Crosses as many bridges owned by that player as it wants, plus one jump per turn.
• <b>Shardbearer:</b> Crosses up to two bridges owned by that player plus one jump per turn.

<color=#ffffff55>────────────────────────────────────────</color>

<size=120%><b>Harvesting Gemhearts</b></size>

To harvest a gemheart on a plateau you must have the <b>majority of troops</b> there, plus either:

• <b>at least 3 troops</b>, or
• <b>a shardbearer</b>.

On a plateau holding both a gemheart and a <b>chasmfiend</b>, you must have the majority <i>and</i> a <b>shardbearer</b> to harvest.

Harvesting happens automatically at the <b>beginning of your turn</b>.

<color=#ffffff55>────────────────────────────────────────</color>

<size=120%><b>Turn Order</b></size>

1. <b>Harvest</b> any gemhearts you qualify for.
2. <b>Buy</b> any pieces you can afford; new pieces spawn on the central plateau.
3. <b>Move</b> each of your troops, parshendi, bridges, and shardbearers one time.

<color=#ffffff55>────────────────────────────────────────</color>

<size=120%><b>Placing Gemhearts</b></size>

Agree on one of the two methods below at the start of the game.

<size=110%><b>Option 1 — Random Placement</b></size>

• Gemhearts are placed randomly across the board.
• Odds are distributed proportionally to plateau size: bigger plateaus are more likely to receive a gemheart, smaller ones less likely.
• Each placement has a <b>33% chance</b> of also spawning a chasmfiend on that plateau.

<size=110%><b>Option 2 — Catapult</b></size>

• A catapult sits just off the edge of the board.
• The player pulls back and aims, firing the gemheart onto the board.
• The gemheart automatically moves to whichever plateau it lands closest to.
• If the gemheart lands <b>completely on a plateau</b> (rather than merely touching it or landing beside it), a chasmfiend is placed there as well.

<color=#ffffff55>────────────────────────────────────────</color>

<size=120%><b>Chasmfiends</b></size>

When a chasmfiend lands on a plateau <b>without a shardbearer</b>:

• All troops on that plateau are <b>removed from the game</b>.
• Any parshendi there must <b>flee to an adjacent plateau</b>.

Troops belonging to a player who has a <b>shardbearer</b> on that plateau may stay.

<color=#ffffff55>────────────────────────────────────────</color>

<size=120%><b>Winning</b></size>

Two agreements must be made before the game begins:

1. The <b>number of gemhearts required to win</b>.
2. The <b>total number of gemhearts in the game</b>.

A player wins immediately upon reaching the agreed gemheart total.

Because the supply is fixed, once <b>all gemhearts have been placed</b>, each player takes <b>one final turn</b>, and the player holding the most gemhearts wins.

<b>Recommended:</b> players × 8 gemhearts in the game, and players × 4 gemhearts to win.
