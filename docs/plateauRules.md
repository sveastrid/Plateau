# Plateau Rules

A VR board game of plateaus, bridges, and gemhearts.

---

## Board Layout

- The board has **40 plateaus**, including one larger **central plateau**.
- Plateaus vary in color. The colors represent the percent chance of that color being chosen.
- A faint line between two plateaus means they can be **crossed by a bridge** or **jumped by a parshendi**.
- All players begin on the central plateau.

---

## VR Interaction

- Each player can see how many gemhearts they currently hold.
- Every piece sits on a colored square indicating its owner.
- Each piece displays a **number** above the piece showing how many of that piece type occupy that plateau.
- Players use a **pointer** to select pieces; the piece highlights when targeted.
- After selecting a piece stack on a plateau, the player uses the **joystick** to choose how many of those pieces to move.
- Plateaus that the selected piece type can legally move to are **highlighted**.

---

## Starting Forces

Each player begins with:

| Piece | Starting count |
|---|---|
| Bridges | 2 |
| Troops | 6 |
| Shardbearer | 1 |
| Parshendi | 2 |

All starting pieces are placed on the central plateau.  Before the game starts, players place their first bridge one at a time, then place the second in reverse order (so that the person who placed the first bridge first will place their second bridge last).

---

## Buying Pieces

| Cost | Purchase |
|---|---|
| 1 gemheart | 2 bridges |
| 1 gemheart | 6 troops |
| 1 gemheart | 2 parshendi |
| 2 gemhearts | 1 shardbearer |

New pieces spawn on the **central plateau**.

---

## Movement

Each piece may move **once per turn**.

| Piece | Movement |
|---|---|
| **Bridge** | One bridge may be moved per turn. A bridge already on the board may be re-laid across any empty gap with one end in the group of plateaus it currently connects — that is, the plateaus reachable from either of its own ends over that player's bridges. A bridge placed from reserve is laid from the group connected to the central plateau. |
| **Troop** | Crosses up to two bridges owned by that player per turn. |
| **Parshendi** | Crosses as many bridges owned by that player as it wants, plus one jump per turn. |
| **Shardbearer** | Crosses up to two bridges owned by that player plus one jump per turn. |

---

## Harvesting Gemhearts

To harvest a gemheart on a plateau you must have the **majority of troops** there, plus either:

- **at least 3 troops**, or
- **a shardbearer**.

On a plateau holding both a gemheart and a **chasmfiend**, you must have the majority *and* a **shardbearer** to harvest.

Harvesting happens automatically at the **beginning of your turn**.

---

## Turn Order

1. **Harvest** any gemhearts you qualify for.
2. **Buy** any pieces you can afford; new pieces spawn on the central plateau.
3. **Move** each of your troops, parshendi, bridges, and shardbearers one time.

---

## Placing Gemhearts

Agree on one of the two methods below at the start of the game.

### Option 1 — Random Placement

- Gemhearts are placed randomly across the board.
- Odds are distributed proportionally to plateau size: bigger plateaus are more likely to receive a gemheart, smaller ones less likely.
- Each placement has a **33% chance** of also spawning a chasmfiend on that plateau.

### Option 2 — Catapult

- A catapult sits just off the edge of the board.
- The player pulls back and aims, firing the gemheart onto the board.
- The gemheart automatically moves to whichever plateau it lands closest to.
- If the gemheart lands **completely on a plateau** (rather than merely touching it or landing beside it), a chasmfiend is placed there as well.

---

## Chasmfiends

When a chasmfiend lands on a plateau **without a shardbearer**:

- All troops on that plateau are **removed from the game**.
- Any parshendi there must **flee to an adjacent plateau**.

Troops belonging to a player who has a **shardbearer** on that plateau may stay.

---

## Winning

Two agreements must be made before the game begins:

1. The **number of gemhearts required to win**.
2. The **total number of gemhearts in the game**.

A player wins immediately upon reaching the agreed gemheart total.

Because the supply is fixed, once **all gemhearts have been placed**, each player takes **one final turn**, and the player holding the most gemhearts wins.

**Recommended:** `players × 8` gemhearts in the game, and `players × 4` gemhearts to win.
